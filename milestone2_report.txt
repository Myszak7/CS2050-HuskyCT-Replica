3. Big-O time complexity for recursive binary search, why is sorting required for correctness:
Worst: O(logn)
Average: O(logn)
Best: O(1), if the item we are searching for is at the median index of the list

A list has to be sorted for a correct recursive binary search because we are "removing" half of the list each time 
we perform a recursive call, and we expect that all elements in that half are either larger or smaller than the value of the 
item we are searching for. This is why we need to call on any of our sorting algorithms to sort the list before we perform
a recursive binary search.
